# Trade Journal

Every trade gets an entry **the day it's opened**. The thesis must be written before
you know the outcome — that's the whole point.

Copy the block below for each trade.

---

## Template

```
### [TICKER] — opened YYYY-MM-DD

Entry:        $XX.XX
Stop:         $XX.XX        (−X.X% / 1R = $20)
Shares:       X.XX          (position $XXX = XX% of account)
Target:       $XX.XX        (+15% → sell half, stop to breakeven)
Earnings:     YYYY-MM-DD    (confirmed via IR page? Y/N)

Market gate:  GREEN / RED
Heat before:  $XX    Heat after: $XX
Positions:    X of 4

Thesis (1 sentence):
    [Why this, why now]

Invalidation (1 sentence):
    [What specifically proves me wrong]

Setup grade before entry:  A / B / C
    (C-grade setups should not be traded. If you're writing C, close the app.)

--- filled in at exit ---
Exit date:    YYYY-MM-DD
Exit price:   $XX.XX
Exit reason:  stop / first target / trail / time stop / thesis break / earnings
P&L:          $XX.XX  =  X.XR
Rules broken: none / [name them]

What actually happened vs the thesis:
    [2 sentences, honest]

Lesson:
    [One line. If there's no lesson, write "none — process worked"]
```

## Running scorecard — update weekly

| Metric | Target | Current |
|---|---|---|
| Trades closed | — | 0 |
| Win rate | 40–50% is normal | — |
| Avg win (R) | ≥ 2.0R | — |
| Avg loss (R) | ≤ 1.0R | — |
| Expectancy | > 0 | — |
| Largest loss | ≤ $20 | — |
| Rule violations | **0** | — |
| Max heat reached | ≤ $60 | — |
| Account | $1,000 start | $— |

**Expectancy = (win% × avg win R) − (loss% × avg loss R)**
Example: 45% × 2.0R − 55% × 1.0R = **+0.35R** — that's a good system.

Note: below ~70 closed trades these numbers are mostly noise. Track them to build the
habit and catch disasters early, not to judge whether the strategy works. That takes
1–3 years of trading at this pace.

## Example (filled in)

```
### ACME — opened 2026-07-14

Entry:        $52.30
Stop:         $48.40        (−7.5% / 1R = $20)
Shares:       5.13          (position $268 = 26.8% of account)
Target:       $60.15        (+15% → sell half, stop to breakeven)
Earnings:     2026-09-04    (confirmed via IR page? Y)

Market gate:  GREEN
Heat before:  $20    Heat after: $40
Positions:    2 of 4

Thesis:
    Broke out of an 8-week base on 2.3x average volume after three tightening
    pullbacks; sector is the strongest of the last month.

Invalidation:
    Closes back below the $51.80 pivot on above-average volume.

Setup grade before entry:  A

--- filled in at exit ---
Exit date:    2026-07-29
Exit price:   $48.40
Exit reason:  stop
P&L:          −$20.01  =  −1.0R
Rules broken: none

What actually happened vs the thesis:
    Breakout failed on day 4 and drifted back through the pivot. Whole sector
    rolled over the same week — I was right about the setup, wrong about the tape.

Lesson:
    Sector strength was already 5 weeks old at entry. Check how mature the sector
    move is, not just that it's leading.
```
